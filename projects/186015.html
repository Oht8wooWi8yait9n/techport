Project Description

This SBIR technology demonstration advances two strategically important capabilities: NanoSonic's lightweight graded-Z radiation shielding composites and Advanced Cooling Technologies' 3D Printed Loop Heat Pipe (3DP LHP). The demonstration will provide flight heritage for both technologies by measuring in-space radiation attenuation using NanoSonic's graded-Z shielding and validating the 3DP LHP's steady-state performance, startup behavior, and long-duration cycling. Both technologies address GSFC ETD Technology Roadmap priorities in thermal management and radiation protection. FY26 effort focuses on final design and fabrication oversight, ensuring GSFC retains technical authority over design decisions and maintains continuous technical engagement with partners as they finalize hardware, setting conditions for FY27 integration ramp-up.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

Flight-demonstrated radiation shielding achieving 2x dose reduction at half the mass density vs. conventional materials, enabling use of high-performance commercial electronics on mass/power-constrained spacecraft; flight-validated 3DP LHP with order-of-magnitude cost reduction and high-performance thermal transport in low SWAP form factor; removal of flight heritage barrier for mission infusion; enablement of higher-performance Artemis and CLPS payloads; resilient lunar and Martian surface systems; pathway to RTMD/ARTD seed funding and hosted payload opportunities
