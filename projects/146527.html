Modeling the Operational Limits of Extreme Environment Electronics for Lunar/Martian Fission Surface Power (FSP)

NASA TechPort Project ID: 146527
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

Performance limits determined through modeling, of wide band gap silicon carbide (SiC) power/instrumentation electronics under concurrent high-temperature, high-radiation conditions for Lunar/Martian Fission Surface Power (FSP) reactors

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

Well-understood performance limits of wide band gap silicon. carbide electronics enable design of reliable power and instrumentation components. Carbide electronics enable design of reliable power and instrumentation components for operation in high temperature, high-radiation environments in Lunar/Martian fission surface power reactors.
