Determining Radiation Hardness of Novel Visible-Blind Photodetectors for UV Astrophysics Applications

NASA TechPort Project ID: 157949
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

GSFC and industry partners are currently pursuing the development of UV-sensitive, visible-blind SiC and AlGaN detectors, which are ideal for UV astrophysics applications, especially broadband near-UV imaging (200-300 nm, e.g., Swift/UVOT, ULTRASAT, UVEX). We will determine whether these UV detectors are sufficiently radiation-hard for space-based applications by carrying out deep irradiation gamma-ray and proton testing.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

The radiation test results would strongly motivate additional development of these exciting technologies for space-based astrophysics applications.

Technology Transition & Outcomes

  • Closed Out: We developed a radiation hardness testing plan for SiC and AlGaN photodiodes to validate their applicability for space-based instrument applications. We adopted a printed circuit board and read-out integrated circuit that enables simultaneous operation of multiple linear and avalanche photodiodes with different voltage requirements while minimizing surface leakage. This setup will be used for future radiation tests. The completion of a project does not necessarily indicate that the development work has stopped. The work could potentially continue in the future as a follow-on IRAD; or be used in collaboration or partnership with Academia, Industry, and other Government Agencies.
