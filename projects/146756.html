Antenna-Coupled MIR/FIR Detectors for High Sensitivity Applications

Project Description

This is an effort to develop antenna-coupled detectors for mid- and far- infrared high sensitivity astrophysics applications. The effort will unite multiple relevant technology achievements to extend this detector technology to higher frequencies and more sensitive detectors.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

Improved sensitivity and greater spectral coverage for mid-infrared and far-infrared detectors suitable for the faint signals in low-background space-based astrophysics instruments.

Technology Transition & Outcomes

  • Closed Out: This project successfully developed two thin-film materials that have high enough conductivity sufficient to make transmission lines to guide electromagnetic energy between a receiving antenna and highly sensitive detectors at terahertz frequencies. Using properties we measured for the new materials, we designed antenna and transmission line structures for efficient coupling between antenna and detector at frequencies higher than previously possible. We developed fabrication processes and detector designs to integrate the new materials with two detector technologies: Kinetic Inductance Detectors and Transition Edge Sensor detectors. We also built new detector testing infrastructure, featuring enhanced cryogenic sources across multiple frequency ranges up to 12 terahertz, designed to provide validation of detectors made with the new materials and designs in a low stray light environment. The completion of a project does not necessarily indicate that the development work has stopped.
