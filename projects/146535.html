1035nm CASALS Lidar for Navigation Velocity and 3-D imaging Measurements

NASA TechPort Project ID: 146535
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

We propose to enhance an 1035nm CASALS lidar system for both navigation Velocity and 3-D imaging Measurements. A recent RI2 project successfully demonstrated the measurement approach at 1550nm. We will tailor the 1035nm CASALS hardware being developed under the CASALS IIP program and experimentally validate both measurements. This project will also investigate a multi-beam CASALS design that could bring significant benefits for navigation and 3-D imaging

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

Hazard avoidance and navigation for lunar landing; Satellite service, space debris clean up, and EDL; 3D mapping for asteroid and comet explorations. Efficient and fast 3D imaging of landing site with 2k x 2k pixels/sec and real-time 3D map generation; Reduce SWaP and cost by allowing long distance velocity and range measurement with same hardware; Relax requirements on key components (steering mirror speed, laser tuning range and speed).
