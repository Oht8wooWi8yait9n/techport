Project Description

The Lunar Oxygen/Water Extraction Project (LOWE) provides groundwork for a long-term consumables and Lunar Highlands Type (LHT) simulant development, and Lunar simulated environmental capabilities strategies. The project seeks to collect information on the state-of-the-art solutions that currently exist and develop low TRL technologies and integrate mature technologies into systems that require lunar consumable solutions.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

Developing technologies for Lunar resource prospecting, utilizing Lunar resources for consumables production, developing low, mid. and high fidelity Lunar simulant, and dust Lunar surface simulation thermal vacuum environment capabilities.
