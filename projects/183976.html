Redesign of EVA Quick Disconnects for Exploration ATCS (QDs)

NASA TechPort Project ID: 183976
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

Design new operator and fluid interface mechanisms to ensure user-friendly and dust-tolerant QDs for Artemis and beyond, increasing stakeholder confidence in critical hardware.​ (TRL-5 at completion o Year 2)

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

New design will address the following SoA ISS ATCS EVA QD design and operational limitations: sliding seals susceptible to dust wear with no particulate purging mechanism, high-risk operational scenarios such as handle kick-back and hydraulic locking due to trapped fluid between redundant seal that require large forces, lengthy procedures, spare tools, and complex training for crew to reliably mate/de-mate. In addition, new QD design will provide new manufacturing capability and address limited spares.
