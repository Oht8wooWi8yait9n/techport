Ultrasonic Measurement of Relative Winds in the Upper Stratosphere (SPARROW)

Project Description

SPARROW (Sensor Package for Attitude, Rotation, and Relative Observable Winds) is a standalone instrumentation package capable of measuring the attitude, rotation, and relative wind encountered by a balloon gondola during ascent, float, and descent phases of flight. SPARROW is a custom-designed package comprised of commercially available sensor technologies modified to work together to measure relative winds at high altitudes.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

SPARROW will measure the relative winds seen by a scientific gondola during a balloon flight. This data can be used to predict wind loading and gondola attitude disturbances for future missions.

Technology Transition & Outcomes

  • Closed Out: For this IRAD effort, a SPARROW prototype was designed, fabricated, and flown on an 11 MCF zero-pressure balloon. The prototype successfully measured the gondola attitude and motion and housekeeping temperatures throughout the flight. The relative wind speed and direction were successfully measured at the gondola altitude during the launch, ascent, and descent phases of flight. The prototype demonstrated that measuring the relative wind during stratospheric balloon flights is viable, but that the prototype will require an improved wind sensor to accommodate stratospheric altitudes.
