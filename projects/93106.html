Materials Manufactured from 3D-Printed Synthetic Biology Arrays: Cell Printing Medium for Synthetic Biomaterial Production

NASA TechPort Project ID: 93106
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

A cell medium designed for 3D printing that allows or enhances production of non-living biomaterials.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

A properly designed cell medium for synthetic biomaterial production maintains cell life support prior to printing, reduces cell stress and damage during printing, and interacts with the cell printing substrate to allow or provide material production stimulus post-printing.

The medium has been developed to allow the printing of 3D arrays of bioengineered cells for the purpose of producing human-designed, non-living biomaterials.  The same medium can, with modifications, support other applications, such as the following:

  • 2D- or 3D-printing of living cells for biological, medical, or pharmaceutical uses
  • controlled growth of material-producing cells, such as biofuels or pharmaceuticals
  • creation, via printing or other means, of biocompatible scaffolds for microbial or tissue growth
