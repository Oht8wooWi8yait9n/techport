Novel Simulant and Regolith Wear Test Capability for Dust Mitigation

NASA TechPort Project ID: 146292
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

Wear behavior of simulants has never been fully tested and compared to regolith behavior to ensure life testing is carried out under realistic conditions. Developing a test method and rig to test wear properties will build the capability in house to test any simulant and comapre it's performance to regolith. Once a test is validated, it can be used for any future simulant variation, and for candidate materials versus known simulants to help develop future materials as well.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

Provide much-needed validation of simulant/regolith wear behavior to enable long-life space mechanisms for planetary exploration and habitation. Current SOA uses simulants as a surrogate for regolith in mechanism life and performance testing without ever having been validated. The proposed effort will test simulant wear properties and enable future validtion with regoltih to inform mechnism designers of proper simulant use for an application.
