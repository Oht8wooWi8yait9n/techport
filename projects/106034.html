Solar System Pony Express, (revised from 2019) (PonyEx)

NASA TechPort Project ID: 106034
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

We propose a global, multi-spectral, high-resolution planetary surveyor supported by regular visits from a cycler satellite network to retrieve petabits of data for transit to Earth. The “courier” satellites use optical communications to receive 1-3 petabits from the surveyor at least once per year. They then travel close to earth where they can downlink the data quickly. By exploiting cycler orbits, the couriers need minimal onboard propulsion and can operate for decades as an augmentation to the Deep Space Network and a precursor to a Human Exploration logistics network.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

Enables global high-resolution survey of planetary body with minimal impact to DSN, yet returns 1000x more data per year from Mars. Easily-engaged commercial sector with “data logistics.” Easily-engaged public interest with visits by couriers which could be built by companies, nations, or schools.
