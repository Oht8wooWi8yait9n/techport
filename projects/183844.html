Deep Space Food Challenge: Mars to Table (M2T)

NASA TechPort Project ID: 183844
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

Building on the success of the Deep Space Food Challenge, the next goal in NASA’s efforts to transform astronaut dietary offerings is focused on incentivizing a complete space food system for a planetary surface that integrates a variety of food sources and associated technologies while meeting 100% of the crew’s variable nutritional needs within the space environment.

Future sustained lunar and deep space missions pose new challenges to crew health, food production, and food preparation. By using open innovation strategies in this area, NASA can incentivize innovative solutions to the challenges of astronaut nutrition. These challenges include food safety, palatability, optimization of resources, and ease of use. The solution may also have Earth-side applications.

The structure of the challenge consists of one level, spanning a total of nine months. Teams will be asked to outfit a Mars habitat space with their proposed food system models informed by subject matter expertise through webinars and office hours. Submissions must include a comprehensive meal plan, a blueprint/layout of the designed space, a detailed concept of operations, and a proposed commercialization strategy that includes evidence for a terrestrial market. all packaged in a submission walkthrough video. Teams will submit their solutions by July 2026, then a judging panel will evaluate submissions and determine winners in September 2026. Up to five total teams may be awarded a portion of the $250,000 prize purse.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

  1. Uses for and integration with habitat and life support systems
  2. Integration of individual production systems to make up a full food system
  3. Selection of foods and food production systems to support nutritional needs
  4. Configuration and layout within the habitat
  5. Uses for various analog testbeds on Earth, in LEO, and on the lunar surface
