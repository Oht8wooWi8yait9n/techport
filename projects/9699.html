Flexible High Energy Lidar Transmitter for Remote Gas and Wind Sensing

NASA TechPort Project ID: 9699
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

Fibertek proposes a high energy and flexible operation 1570 nm pulsed lidar transmitter for airborne and space-based remote CO2 gas and doppler wind sensing. The lidar system is expected to operate with 5 mJ energy at 1 kHz repetition rate, and the pulse width will be adjustable from 20 ns to 1 microseconds. The proposed system is based on Fibertek's proprietary laser architecture that utilize state-of-the-art optical and RF analog and digital electronic component technologies.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

1-Scientific tool for Global Warming and biological Greenhouse Gas research 2-Monitoring wind profiles around airports and wind turbines 3-Tracking and monitoring dispersion of CO2 and other hazardous plume at industrial sites 4-Airborne wind mapping for aviation hazard detection and flight pattern optimization

1-ASCENDS mission Lidar transmitter CO2 for concentration and wind sensing 2-Airborne and ground operation for local mapping of CO2 and wind profile dynamics
