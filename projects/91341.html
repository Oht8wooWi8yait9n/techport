Testing ON-OFF Gecko Adhesive Grippers in Microgravity

NASA TechPort Project ID: 91341
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

JPL has developed a tool for grappling non-cooperative objects in microgravity using switchable adhesives that are inspired by the gecko lizard. Using these gecko-like adhesives, which can be turned ON and OFF and reused many times, the tool grapples objects by virtually any surface, significantly reducing the requirements on other systems like perception, GN&C, and control during the grapple sequence.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

This technology is a tool for grappling non-cooperative objects and may have such applications as satellite servicing, exterior inspection of on-orbit vehicles and orbital debris mitigation. The use of directional adhesion to effectively handle objects that are difficult or impossible to manipulate with gripping tools will benefit the commercial space industry, future NASA missions, and other government agencies such as the Department of Defense.
