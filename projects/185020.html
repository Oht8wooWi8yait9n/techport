Advanced Mid-Power Electric Rocket (AMPERE)

Project Description

Advanced Mid-Power Electric Rocket (AMPERE) is a 16-month study that leverages past NASA R&D investments and commercialization lessons learned to catalyze development of domestic cost-effective mid-power Hall-effect thrusters (HET).  It's objective is to develop a design to enable a domestic commercial product that is cost-effective for industry and capable enough for NASA.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

  • Addresses an urgent market gap for flight-proven mid-power HETs in which foreign suppliers dominate, with averse national security and geopolitical impacts
  • Engages industry stakeholders early during thruster specification formulation and conceptual design to secure commercial buy-in
  • Supports extensibility to NASA mission infusion opportunities while avoiding pitfalls that have resulted in limited prior commercialization in previous NASA-led electric propulsion development efforts
