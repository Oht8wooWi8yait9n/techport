Project Description

Under the Propellant Production Plant Tipping Point (Pro-TP) project, Blue Origin's goal is to build and test a full-scale In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU) liquid oxygen/liquid hydrogen (LO2/LH2) liquefaction and storage prototype testbed that could be used or replicated on the first flight Blue Moon descent lander. This test bed would use full-scale Blue Moon hardware and commercial cryocooler systems. The initial contract focuses on the more difficult LH2 system, with a potential contract modification to add the LO2 side as budget allows.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

The proposed program pulls together all the critical technologies that NASA and industry
have been developing for years into a full-scale O2/H2 demonstration for the final
push to tip lunar propellant liquefaction towards routine operation. At TRL6 there will be high
confidence in our ability to produce propellant reliably.
