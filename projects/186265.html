Water vapor Infrared Sunlight Sounder with Photonic integrated circuits (WISSP)

NASA TechPort Project ID: 186265
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

WISSP aims to develop a photonic integrated circuit (PIC)-based spectrometer/polarimeter for measurement of the 3D distribution of water vapor from orbit. WISSP will be smaller and more accurate than current IR sounders, while providing cross-track coverage to supplement high-resolution lidar.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

PIC technology enables CubeSat-sized instrument (3D maps + constellation). Develop, demonstrate, and characterize improved TRL 4 prototype suitable for brassboard implementation
