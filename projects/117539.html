View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

The goal of Phase 2 is to further the development of technologies that can excavate and transport large quantities of icy lunar regolith and can address the technology gaps such as excavating and delivering large quantities of icy regolith, development of reliable and durable hardware that is lightweight and energy efficient, and can operate well in extreme lunar environmental conditions. Through a prototype demonstration, Teams must show that their solutions address the reliability, durability, and traversability challenges these systems must overcome to operate for long durations.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

THis challenge is designed to develop new technologies that could support a sustained human presence on the Moon by the end of the decade.

Technology Transition & Outcomes

  • Advanced From: Phase 1 closed. Phase 2 funs from June 2022 - Dec. 2023. Phase 1 is Long Duration Testing. Prize Purse is $2M.
  • Advanced From
  • Closed Out: Phase 2 is completed June 2024. Phase 2 contained 3 levels. Level 1 closed in December 2022. NASA awarded 13 US teams an equal share of $500K by judging the prototype design reports and long duration test plans. Nine teams from Level 1 competed in Level 2. In Level 2, Teams attempted and/or performed 15-day long testing of their prototypes. Level 2 closed in December 2023. NASA awarded a total of $850K to six teams based on the test reports and by considering the mass and energy efficiency of the prototype robotics excavation and transportation systems. Level 3 concluded on June 12, 2024, and NASA awarded $1.5M in cash prizes and three Thermal Vacuum Chamber test opportunities. Two teams (Star Path and Terra Engineering) elected to participate in the Thermal Vacuum Chamber testing.
