Polaris Project: Enabling Full Scale Laser Procressed Condensing Heat Exchanger (LP-CHX) Manufacturing

NASA TechPort Project ID: 116430
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

Enabling Capabilities special project series: Polaris Project, small flight experiments or risk reduction projects to fulfill high-priority capabilities gaps.

Project Description & Objectives

  • Development of a novel electroplating process to reduce LP-CHX manufacturing complexities, ultimately decreasing manufacturing time and cost by 18 months and >$1M
  • Deliverables of this project will be 1-3 full-scaled electroplated packets, microbial test, and a detailed LP-CHX manufacturing plan

Why this Project is Important & Tier 1

Gap Addressed

  • Current CHX technology relies on a coating which prematurely loses its hydrophilicity leading to water carryover and ultimately premature CHX refurbishment
  • Tier 1 Gap 06-50: Robust CHX’s enabling future crewed missions

Technology Infusion & Innovation Approach

The LP-CHX technology is highly favored by Habitation Systems, AES, and ECLSS SCLT and vetted in a May ‘20 CHX down-select against two other CHX technologies. Potential infusion is ultimately on an ISS technology demonstration or integrated ECLSS Pallet Demo at MSFC
