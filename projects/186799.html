Intelligent Multi-Agent Constellations for Cooperative Cislunar Operations

NASA TechPort Project ID: 186799
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio

Project Description

Future missions to cislunar space and beyond will rely on satellite constellations to support navigation, communication, and operations. To reduce costs and operational burdens while increasing mission versatility, these constellations must be capable of autonomous, cooperative, and strategic decision-making under the highly nonlinear dynamics of the Earth-Moon system. However, existing constellation design methods typically rely on low-fidelity dynamical modeling or neglect the autonomous decision-making structures required for reconfigurable multi-agent operations. This research proposes a framework for intelligent cislunar satellite constellations that integrates dynamical systems theory with cooperative multi-agent control methods. By leveraging quasi-periodic orbits (QPOs) arising in cislunar models and recent parameterization methods that transform these nonlinear dynamics and trajectories into a linear representation on an invariant torus, cooperative dynamic games can be used to autonomously coordinate and reconfigure constellations to efficiently complete shared objectives. The project is structured into three phases. In the first phase, parameterized QPO dynamics will be leveraged to design constellations and ensure bounded relative motion. In the second phase, dynamic game-theoretic methods will be integrated to find control policies that enable autonomous formation reconfiguration and cooperative objective completion. In the third phase, these control policies and dynamical structures will be transitioned across a hierarchy of cislunar models with the goal of validating under high-fidelity ephemeris models. The resulting framework aims to enable mission-adaptive autonomy for future cislunar satellite constellations, directly supporting NASA's objectives for sustained lunar exploration and operations.
