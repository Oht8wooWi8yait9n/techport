Project Description

Astrobotics revolutionary Spacerless Multi-layer Insulation enclosures (SMLI) mark a paradigm shift in thermal isolation capabilities, offering an order of magnitude improvement over traditional multilayer insulation systems.The integration of SMLI within Astrobotics Commercial Lunar Payload Services (CLPS) landers introduces a cost-effective approach to enhance power efficiency and reduce battery mass, crucial considerations in the demanding thermal conditions of PSRs. SMLI significantly contributes to the operational efficiency and extended mission longevity of CLPS landers in these challenging lunar environments.SMLIwill benefit a diverse range of customerswith a particular emphasis on its applicability in the challenging thermal environments like Cis-Lunar transit, Lunar Night and operations in Permanently Shadowed Regions (PSRs) on the moon. In the dynamic domain of space exploration, the demand for pioneering solutions to optimize spacecraft performance is more crucial than ever. One of the major challenges imposed on spacecraft developers stems from the extreme thermal environments encountered in space. As NASA and the commercial industrial base continue to advance the boundaries of exploration through the Moon to Mars campaign, operating thermally efficient devices in cislunar space and on the lunar surface during the lunar night have become increasingly critical. Astrobotic is using its 17-year history developing and deploying spacecraft in cislunar and lunar surface environments to address this problem by proposing its Spacer-less Multi-layer Insulation (SMLI) enclosure, which will provide an order of magnitude improvement in thermal performance over traditional multilayer insulation systems and subsequently drive major reductions in spacecraft battery mass. 

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

The incorporation of SMLI, particularly in missions involving lunar night exploration and operations in Permanently Shadowed Regions, offers significant economic benefits, enabling NASA to utilize Astrobotic's services for scientific endeavors with enhanced cost efficiency.  Offering a turnkey solution, SMLI can be seamlessly integrated into the systems and development projects of commercial partners, research institutes, and other entities involved in space exploration, providing a significant advantage in thermal management for spacecraft operations in these scientifically and commercially valuable lunar areas.
