Ensuring High Performance μ-Spec Spectrometer Technology Reaches Fruition

NASA TechPort Project ID: 117129
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

This works is to progress integrated on-chip far-infrared spectrometer technology towards the higher performance required for next generation far-infrared balloon or space astrophysics instruments.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

Demonstration of a moderate resolution far-infrared on-chip spectrometer.

Technology Transition & Outcomes

  • Closed Out: We have progressed integrated on-chip far-infrared spectrometer technology towards the performance required for next generation far-infrared balloon or space astrophysics instruments. We developed high performance component designs and integrated those designs into a complete spectrometer system design. We developed a full fabrication process flow for this design. We developed and completed the critical microfabrication process steps necessary to yield on-chip spectrometers with science grade performance. The completion of a project does not necessarily indicate that the development work has stopped. The work could potentially continue in the future as a follow-on IRAD; or be used in collaboration or partnership with Academia, Industry, and other Government Agencies. Goddard Space Flight Center’s IRAD program is managed under its Office of Chief Technologist. Activities develop concepts, reduce technology risk, and advance human capital and technological capabilities. Goddard’s IRAD program is highly competitive, opportunity-driven, and 100% strategically aligned with NASA's and Goddard's strategic priorities. A significant portion of the program is focused on Early Stage Innovations for high-risk, strategically aligned, potential high-payoff technologies that are longer-term or lower TRL.
