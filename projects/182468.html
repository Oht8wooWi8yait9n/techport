Lunar Dust Reduction through Electrostatic Adhesion Mitigation  (L-DREAM)

NASA TechPort Project ID: 182468
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

L-DREAM seeks to develop a passive, transparent lunar dust mitigation coating for application on solar cells and thermal control surfaces that works by minimizing electrostatic adhesion forces. The project also seeks to develop coating compatibility with large-scale manufacturing methods and solutions for synergistic use of the coating with active dust mitigation strategies.

Technical Objectives
1. Determine environmental durability of the L-DREAM coating
2. Determine how the L-DREAM coating will affect the operation of its application technologies through testing it with solar cells, thermal control surfaces, and electrodynamic dust shield
3. Demonstrate L-DREAM dust reduction in a simulated lunar environment at the coupon-scale
4. Demonstrate L-DREAM coating compatibility with large-scale manufacturing methods
5. Demonstrate dust mitigation of the L-DREAM coating on a solar array test article and thermal control surfaces

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

Anticipated L-DREAM coating benefits

  • Maintain high efficiency and increase lifetime in lunar solar cells and thermal control surfaces despite dusty conditions
  • Reduce solar array area needed to meet end of life (EOL) power requirements by minimizing power losses due to dust coverage
  • Save costs and time related to maintenance, manual dust cleaning, and replacement of damaged parts
  • Provide a technology ready to be licensed to aerospace industry for application onto a variety of commercial space systems
