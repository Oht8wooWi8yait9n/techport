3D Printed Habitat - Structural Member Competition

NASA TechPort Project ID: 94154
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

Structural Member: Demonstrate a recycling additive manufacturing material system that can create structural components using terrestrial/space based materials and recyclables.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

The long-term vision is that habitat-manufacturing machines could someday be deployed to the Moon, Mars or beyond to autonomously prepare shelters for the human explorers who follow.
