Project Description

Developing remote mobile dexterous manipulation capabilities for robust IVR and surface robot task performance.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

Enables broad, robust manipulation capabilities for intravehicular and exploration robotics deployable to multiple manipulator platforms. An established remote manipulation capability provides for the immediate utility of IVR and surface systems when these robots come online. Critical IVR and surface manipulation tasks are made possible in the absence of human crew or crew time. Semi-autonomous behaviors completed through this effort provide the needed starting point for further automation and autonomous operations in the future.
