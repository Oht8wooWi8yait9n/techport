Project Description

The Distributed Sensing activity (TTT project) and SmartMobility project at NASA Ames research center (ARC) conducted a flight test campaign in November-December 2021. The goal of the ARC flight test campaign was to obtain high-definition RGB camera images and 3D scanning LiDAR data to support multiple studies. These studies include airborne autonomy for hazard perception, safe beyond-visual-line-of-sight operations, GPS-free navigation, and real-time mapping. In this campaign, the following dataset was collected at a low altitude of <100m, targeting different suburban/urban structures of interest, including roads, buildings, trees, people, vehicles, and vegetation.

Payload specifications:

  • Velodyne VLP16 3D Scanning LiDAR
  • Xsens MTI-20 VRU IMU
  • Intel NUC i7 CPU
  • FLIR DUO-R Thermal IR
  • FLIR 4K Visual Camera

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

This data provides anticipated benefit for development efforts targeting sensor fusion, image processing & computer vision, autonomy technology, and navigation in urban environments.

Technology Transition & Outcomes

  • Closed Out: These flight tests were supported by ARMD through TACP and the Transformative Tools and Technologies program and facilitated by the Revolutionary Aviation Mobility subproject and Autonomous Systems Discipline. After viewing the data, the PI concluded that the flight was a success and produced the following data for public consumption for the benefit of computer vision algorithm development and autonomy research.
