Solar White Coating Development

NASA TechPort Project ID: 158454
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

This project's objective was to develop the Solar White coating material and application through coupon testing at NASA facilities. To accomplish this objective, the project developed technology to manufacture solar reflector tiles of Yttrium Oxide and Barium Fluoride and mature the technology to use as a spray-on solar reflector coating.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

Solar reflectors hold promise to serve a critical role in the passive maintenance of cryogens such as liquid oxygen or liquid methane in deep-space storage. Solar White can be applied in a variety of ways including a spray and tile application, depending on mission needs.

Technology Transition & Outcomes

  • Closed Out: The Solar White activity was originally started in 2015 under a NASA Innovative Advanced Concepts (NIAC) award given to KSC Engineering. The activity primary objective was to develop a novel, high reflectivity coating that could be applied to cryogenic systems as a passive thermal layer. An example application would be coating the external surface of a cryogenic tank, with the coating acting effectively as an insulation layer reducing the heat load going into the tank. KSC Engineering analyses showed coating application to cryogenic tank surfaces could potentially achieve cryogenic temperatures in deep space, essentially reflecting enough of the Sun’s irradiant power, while emitting thermal power, to achieve steady state temperatures below 90 K. KSC Engineering conducted coating material development and eventually patented the coating formula designated ‘Solar White.’ Since 2015, the Solar White development work has continued under additional NIAC awards as well as funding from KSC Launch Services Program (LSP), Game Changing Development (GCD), and other sources. In 2021, the Solar White activity was incorporated into the CFMPP office structure under the Technologies Portfolio led by GRC. The activity’s scope, schedule, resources, and performance metrics were baselined consistent with the CFMPP office’s activity definition requirements. The Principal Investigator was Robert Youngquist of KSC Engineering, who was succeeded by Mark Nurge of KSC Engineering. GRC was designated as the Project lead center for this activity within CFMPP. Mr. Nurge completed the activity scope with a final report delivered to NASA / GRC and the CFMPP office. The final report designated ‘CFT-RPT-0015: Cryogenic Thermal Coating Final Report, provided a comprehensive review of the Solar White development work since 2015 and results from all coating development and testing through 2023. CFMPP performed a Technology Readiness Level (TRL) assessment of the Solar White technology, assigning a value of 4 achieved through all the completed work. CFMPP closed out the Solar White activity, including documentation of final activity reports and data on the CFMPP database, in February 2024.
  • Infused To (United Launch Alliance): United Launch Alliance (ULA) has included solar white in its tipping point project and has a RSAA to develop and test a version to eventually coat a flight Centaur tank.
