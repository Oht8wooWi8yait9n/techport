Project Description

All lunar systems will require dust mitigation strategies, whether they be operations/architectural, passive or active technologies. The Lunar Surface Innovation Initiative (LSII) Dust Mitigation Project provides groundwork for a long-term dust mitigation strategy. The Project collects information on the state-of-the-art solutions that currently exist (via market analysis and development of a Best Practices Guide Book), develops NASA standards for classifying the definition, testing and mitigation of dust, develops low Technology Readiness Level (TRL) technologies (such as piezoelectric-driven dust, plasma/e-beam dust lofting, and electrodynamic dust repellers/collectors, coatings and soft polymeric materials) and integrate mature technologies into systems that require dust mitigation solutions (such as Electrostatic Dust Shield & Patch Plate Materials). This project is a portfolio of the following 6 tasks:

  • Task 1 - Patch Plate Materials Compatibility Assessment
  • Task 2 - Electrodynamic Dust Shield (EDS) for the Lunar Environment
  • Task 3 - Dust Tolerant Mechanisms
  • Task 4 - Dust Mitigation Best Practices Guide
  • Task 5 - Lunar Occupancy Dust-Surface Separation Technologies (LO-DuSST)
  • Task 6 - Dusty Environments Classifications

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

    • Reduces health hazard associated with incidental dust exposure
    • Improves functioning and increases life of equipment
    • Enables broader array of missions on the lunar surface, longer lifetime for deployed systems, and a greater probability of mission success
    • Defines clear dust standards that systems must withstand and qualify to
    • Provides a comprehensive guide for avoiding lunar dust problems and the current state-of-knowledge to all stakeholders ``
