WFF 6 Port Ethernet Switch (WFFES-6)

NASA TechPort Project ID: 157664
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

The WFFES6 project plans to produce a lightweight, low-cost, purpose-built six port Ethernet Switch capable of surviving the environments encountered during suborbital projects like Sounding Rockets and Balloons.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

This project plans to produce the following benefits:

  • An open-source platform that allows custom firmware to be implemented to reduce the technological barrier of entry to ethernet capable devices.
  • An open-source platform that would allow ease of understanding of firmware/operational capability required to develop ethernet capable devices.
  • A low cost device that would enable low-budget programs to fly ethernet capable hardware without having to justify the expense of COTS hardware.

Technology Transition & Outcomes

  • Closed Out: Prototype units built and tested to show that 1000Mbps throughput on all ethernet interfaces was possible with no errors detected. Prototype testing highlighted errors in electrical design and due to parts shortage power conditioning circuitry was not implemented. Able to show that host controller can interface with the network switch component for future expansion into low level management capabilities. Was able to develop 3 boards and a single enclosure including NRE costs. Project determined highest cost item was the mechanical enclosure and initiated trade study to see if a modular enclosure can be designed to support similar form-factor WFF based developments to take advantage of economy of scale and less custom work per enclosure. Project was able to show that a ruggedized low-cost unmanaged layer 2 switch can be developed if cost of mechanical enclosures can be reduced. The completion of a project does not necessarily indicate that the development work has stopped. The work could potentially continue in the future as a follow-on IRAD; or be used in collaboration or partnership with Academia, Industry, and other Government Agencies.
