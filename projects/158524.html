Alternative Cryocooler Development

NASA TechPort Project ID: 158524
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

The goal of the Alternative Cryocooler development activity is to broaden industry base and develop 20K and 90K Cryocooler alternatives - both various cycle and vendor alternatives - that can be infused into lunar and Mars missions. The activity will develop technology alternatives to existing Reverse Turbo-Brayton cycle 20K and 90K cryocooler systems, assess alternate concepts / supplier for components, and provide investment to continue the development of 90K and 20K cryocooler systems to a flight ready status of TRL 6.

Specific activity objectives include: 

  • In-house Government design of Cryocooler system concepts capable of:
    • 25-50 Watts Lift at 20K temperature.
    • 30-150 Watts Lift at 90K temperature.
    • Operation within in-space environments.
  • Cryocooler system demonstration to TRL-6 through conducting ground tests in thermal-vacuum and vibration at representative flight system environments.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

The benefit of the Alternative Cryocooler activity is to develop alternative technologies and suppliers for cryocoolers needed for long duration human exploration missions using cryogenic propellants. High Capacity Cryocoolers are deemed to be an enabling technology for the long-duration storage of cryogens requiring a robust technological and industrial base to support future missions. The Reverse Turbo-Brayton (RTB) cycle addresses the need for zero boil off storage of propellant cryogens critical to lunar and Mars in-space and surface architectures.
