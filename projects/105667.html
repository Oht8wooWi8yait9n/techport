High Efficiency 6kW Dual Mode Electric Propulsion Engine for Broad Mission Applications (6kW EP)

NASA TechPort Project ID: 105667
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

This project will expand SSL's electric propulsion capabilities by developing a selectable "dual mode" power processing unit (PPU) capable of providing 300 or 600 volts to a 6 kilowatt Hall thruster, increasing overall mission efficiency and flexibility. This provides faster, more efficient, propulsive capabilities for future NASA missions.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

Both NASA and Maxar benefit from this tipping point. Maxar's benefits include being able to take advantage of a thrust mode (get to orbit as possible) and a high specific impulse mode (be as fuel efficient as possible)while on oribt. NASA's benefits include more capability to perform high delta V, low thrust missions, launch vehicle savings, and mission extenstion at lower costs.

Technology Transition & Outcomes

  • Infused To (Exploration Systems Development Mission Directorate): Technology will be used by HEO's Gateway Power and Propulsion Element. "The 6kW electric propulsion thruster for Power and Propulsion Element (PPE) started as a Maxar and Busek IR&D program to establish a domestic supplier for Maxar’s Electric Propulsion (EP) spacecraft to replace the current Fakel (Russia) SPT-140 EP thrusters used on Maxar’s GEOCommSats. Maxar included the 6kW EP system in their PPE proposal as part of their partner objectives to demonstrate a next-generation (domestic sourced) EP thruster for commercial applications.“ - Shahab, Chaudhry, Project Manager for PPE.
