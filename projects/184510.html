30W / 90K Cryocooler Development

NASA TechPort Project ID: 184510
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

Design, build, and demonstrate a 90 Kelvin 30 Watt Cryocooler System including the mechanical and control electronics.  A high fidelity prototype will be designed, built, and demonstrated under this SBIR Phase III contract.  This scale is suitable for a flight demonstration and incorporates lesson's learned and advancements achieved in prior Reverse Turbo-Brayton Cryocooler development and demonstration contracts for 90 Kelvin and 20 Kelvin applications resulting in a TRL-6 integrated system ready for infusion into a flight demonstration.  

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

A 90K 30W  cryocooler is necessary to demonstrate in-space ZBO Liquid Hydrogen storage using 2-stage cooling to TRL-7. This development will achieve TRL-6 demonstration of a complete RTB cryocooler system including TMU and CCE.
