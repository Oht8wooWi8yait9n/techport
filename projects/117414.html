Gandalf’s Staff Walking Stick for Lunar EVA Support and Surface Science

NASA TechPort Project ID: 117414
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio

Project Description

Gandalf’s Staff is a concept based upon the Apollo program requirements for lunar surface tools to aid EVAs (see http://apollo17.org?t=144:50:39), photogrammetry (Apollo used color corrections with gnomon), and a lunar surveying system (LSS) including a monopod, television camera, film camera, penetrometer, and laser tracker (not implemented but targeted for later [cancelled] Apollo missions). This proposed Artemis Program conceptual design includes 360° LiDAR and cameras, LED lighting, Sample tag dispenser, Gnomon with calibration bars, and multiple surface contact instruments (e.g. spectrometer, seismic geophone, penetrometer), with supporting communications and navigation equipment.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

The Gandalf's Staff Walking Stick for Lunar EVA Support and Surface Science project directly leads to knowledge acquisition for two ADP focus areas: #6 Lunar sampling R&D for Reqts & Approach (sampling, containing, transporting volatile/cryogenic samples) #7 Surface science: prioritization, planning and ops prioritization development & integration The Apollo program identified a need for an EVA monopod to assist astronauts on lunar traverses, augmented with navigation, communication and science instruments. The current state-of-the-art for 360° LiDAR and camera technology provides for rendering 3D models of spatial geometry but not integrated in a mobile deice that supports multi-purposes (science, illumination, communication and navigation equipment). The proposed tool revolutionizes the documentation of traverse execution and sample collection benefitting the EVA and science community tremendously.
