ICE CHILL: Integrated Cryo-operable Electronics to Condition Hibernation for Improved Li-ion Longevity

NASA TechPort Project ID: 158440
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

 ICE CHILL, a novel integrated battery module/battery management system
(BMS) and demonstrate its capability to enable passive Li-ion battery hibernation through the lunar 
night with zero excess degradation caused by freezing the cells. We will deliver a cryogenically 
operable battery management system, a 28V/15Ah battery module that can passively survive the 
lunar night, and a comprehensive Li-ion battery hibernation protocol for other future designs to 
follow. ICE CHILL builds upon past Center Innovation Fund (CIF) research projects, where we 
demonstrated that commercial-off-the-shelf (COTS) Li-ion cells and control electronics can 
survive a single lunar night without degradation.
 

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

The Commercial Lunar Payload Services (CLPS) program was 
always envisioned as a service providing resources for more than one lunar day. Indeed, the third 
Payloads and Research Investigations on the Surface of the Moon (PRISM-3) call will have a 
threshold survival of two lunar days, with survival through a lunar night for any payload provider 
respondents. ICE CHILL directly addresses the near term needs for CLPS landers to survive 
the lunar night by providing lunar night survivable battery systems for energy storage needs.
