Laser Embedded Drill Bits for Planetary and Terrestrial Exploration

NASA TechPort Project ID: 183998
Official NASA Source: Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) TechPort Portfolio
View Official Project on NASA TechPort →

Project Description

We are partnering with Blue Origin’s Honeybee Robotics to develop exploration drills with embedded laser technology that can identify material in real time while operating below the surface. For planetary exploration, this capability will speed up the discovery of water, minerals, and potential biosignatures on the Moon and Mars. On Earth, this will enable more precise discovery of mining sites, offering real-world payoff as demand for rare earth materials continues to grow rapidly.

Anticipated Benefits & Mission Applications

Creates drill-embedded LIBS spectroscopy system for real-time subsurface material identification; Enables depth-resolved analysis of planetary resources with compact laser systems; Provides modular and scalable technology for various drilling applications
