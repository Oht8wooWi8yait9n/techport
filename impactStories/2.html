Terrain Relative Navigation: Touchdown On Rocky New Worlds

Impact Story ID: 2
Official NASA Impact Story: Following STMD-funded technologies from concept to flight infusion and commercialization.
Read Official Impact Story on NASA TechPort →

Summary: Terrain Relative Navigation (TRN), a technology developed by NASA, facilitates increased landing accuracy and helps spacecraft avoid surface hazards. It allows the exploration of other worlds, like Mars and various moons, in areas that were previously considered too difficult for the spacecraft to land.
