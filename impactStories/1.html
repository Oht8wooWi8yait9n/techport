Roll-Out Solar Arrays: Rolling Out a Path to Future Space Travel

Impact Story ID: 1
Official NASA Impact Story: Following STMD-funded technologies from concept to flight infusion and commercialization.
Read Official Impact Story on NASA TechPort →

Summary: Roll-Out Solar Arrays (ROSA) are an alternative to existing solar array technologies. These arrays are a compact design, more affordable, and offer autonomous capabilities that can enhance a wide spectrum of scientific and commercial missions, from low-Earth orbit all the way to interplanetary travel.
